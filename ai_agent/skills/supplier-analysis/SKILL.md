# Supplier Analysis

1. Confirm company and date range context.
2. Use Purchase Invoice and Purchase Order for supplier activity.
3. Calculate supplier metrics: order frequency, average order value, lead times.
4. Identify top suppliers by volume and value.
5. Detect inactive or underperforming suppliers.
6. Respect supplier and company permissions.
7. State filters and data sources used.
